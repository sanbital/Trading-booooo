## Changed contract

`coordinator.mjs` issues an unforgeable in-process lifecycle capability only after a valid initial dispatch approval. The first confirmed IOC finality arms a 15-second deadline. Signal object, ticket identity and original target remain bound; the token cannot survive another process/cycle, be serialized, cloned or consumed twice.

`gpt-final-review-adapter.mjs` requires sequence 2 and a fresh detector record. No-change evidence can use that capability beyond the original approval/trigger clock. A triggered FINAL recheck still requires its own valid BUY; SKIP, ABSTAIN, API errors, malformed responses and stale FINAL answers fail closed.

`index.ts` refreshes tape, two-sided depth, symbol filters, account/portfolio/order evidence, margin, target and remaining quantity. Retry worst-case total margin is capped at 150 USDT, including the first partial fill. The planner retains its 12 bps chase/slippage and 25 bps catastrophic-spread bounds. Partial fills require native protection before top-up. A second partial/zero fill terminates; a third attempt is refused before creating an intent. Authority and quote age are checked again after durable intent/lease I/O, immediately before the exchange call. An un-dispatched intent is explicitly rejected.

The zero-fill reconciliation branch now validates the existing protected partial position for a second zero fill instead of incorrectly demanding an entirely flat portfolio. Open-position management and exit/protection policy implementations are unchanged. Leverage 3, target margin 150 and global MAX_SLOTS 10 are unchanged.

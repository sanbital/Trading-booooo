# Monthly cost profile

Historical profile below; superseded on 2026-09-28. Current caps are OpenAI
USD100/month and DeepSeek USD100/month, both enforced independently. Daily
USD50/100 values are secondary ceilings. Fixed Top10 periodic review is ten minutes.
See [current production evidence](CAMPAIGN_EXECUTION_20260928.md). Do not restore
these historical values through an old release script.

User authorization, 2026-09-27: AI plus incremental storage must stay within USD 50/month. Existing server fees are separate; optimize Fly and storage as well. The earlier proposed USD 5,000/day increase was never applied and is superseded.

- AI: USD 45 per UTC calendar month, USD 1.25 per UTC day, 100 review jobs/day maximum. A review job may call GPT FIRST, DeepSeek and GPT FINAL, so jobs are not individual provider requests. Claims are serialized across the monthly ledger; completed known costs settle in full, unknown costs keep the reservation. Provider request byte/output limits bound one three-provider job below its USD 0.25 reservation at the configured price cards.
- The monthly total includes self-evolution and legacy Leader/Emerging research ledgers, including uncertain reservations. Optional new paid research and 13 unused legacy shadow schedules are disabled; old evidence, cached policy and live safety monitoring remain. The self-evolution worker cadence is five minutes. Settled shadow cost replaces its reservation instead of counting both.
- Half the daily budget/call pool is held for production position management and FINAL RECHECK. Paid emergency advice uses the same ledger. Budget exhaustion preserves native hard protection and denies new exposure; it does not create an automatic strategic EXIT.
- Held positions keep five-second capture and native protection. Stable periodic AI review moves to six hours; ordinary changed-evidence reviews have a five-minute minimum gap and urgent multi-axis failure/BTC shock/liquidity collapse/hard-stop proximity a one-minute gap. The first post-fill review is immediate when no prior review exists. Up to 12 reviews/position/hour may request the shared budget; neither this ceiling nor an elapsed timer grants EXIT authority.
- On the changeover date, previous usage is retained in the monthly total. The daily allowance starts at the existing daily reservation/call baseline; these offsets expire automatically at UTC midnight. No ledger reset or retrospective credit is performed.
- Source ranking: complete numeric rolling-24h Top20 is retained as an audit record. The active observation subset is ranks 1–10, refreshed every six hours. Held/unsettled symbols outside it and the BTC context sensor remain captured. Legacy candidates remain captured only while the legacy strategy still owns entry.
- Capture remains 5 seconds × 24 buckets. Entry review requests are coalesced, at most one globally every 30 minutes; a symbol requires 30 minutes between evidence-triggered reviews and gets fair scheduling after six hours. Actual paid reviews are lower when data, budget, or exposure constraints apply. The scheduler cannot authorize an order. This deliberately replaces the earlier frequent all-20 AI polling to meet the user's monthly limit.
- Scheduler calculations are at most once per minute; capture itself continues every five seconds. The observation Edge invocation drops from every 20 seconds to every minute.
- A valid ENTRY DEFER finishes its review event immediately. Leader campaigns cannot enter the old seconds-long WAIT retry chain, including after process restart. The next paid decision needs a new paced event; technical timeout recovery remains bounded and separately budgeted.
- Private raw retention: at most 4 GiB hot DB, 72 hours; at most 8 GiB compressed object storage, 30 days. Only Top10 + held/unsettled + BTC raw micro rows are archived. A lease-owned object is uploaded without overwrite, downloaded, decompressed and SHA-256 checked before becoming eligible for hot deletion. Object expiry is 30 days after its newest row. Failed export or checksum cannot delete the source.
- Reuse the existing capture Edge Function for archival every two minutes. It may finish one expired-object deletion then one upload. No extra Fly machine, disk volume, paid replica or separate trading bot is added. Existing capture/gateway hardware is audited during release. Continuous public WebSocket capture needs a running process; native order protection must not depend on waking a stopped machine.
- The existing collector was reduced from shared 4 CPU/1024 MB to shared 1 CPU/256 MB with ten continuous healthy minutes and a real six-hour universe rotation verified. The gateway already uses shared 1 CPU/256 MB. Both have zero mounted volumes.
- The USD 5 incremental-storage allowance is a planning reserve, not a provider billing limit. At documented storage/disk prices the configured 4/8 GiB maxima are well below that allowance; actual provider billing also includes provisioned disk steps and shared-organization usage. Do not claim a provider invoice was verified from logical table bytes alone.

Deployment requires passing Node/PostgreSQL and Deno tests, exact downloaded Edge source parity, authenticated private-storage roundtrip and resource audit. Strategy activation is a separate audited step after readiness and original-flow checks. A valid GPT ENTER and fresh FINAL RECHECK are still required for any actual order; no forced verification orders.

Open positions and pending settlement pin their symbols' hot evidence and every matching cold object. Each object stores its complete symbol manifest before hot deletion; an unknown manifest cannot authorize deletion. A database operator may set `retention_pinned=true` on a PENDING/VERIFIED object before starting a replay that needs it, then release the pin after the replay. A deletion already claimed cannot be retroactively pinned. Service-role callers cannot edit these pins. Retention pins never increase the 4/8 GiB caps: exhausted capacity still pauses new strategy entry and preserves evidence and position protection.
